# CRP — CONTROLLED RECOVERY PERIOD

> Purpose: 15–20 minutes of deep rest. Not sleep — a deliberate recovery state.
> Aligned with the body's natural 90-minute ultradian rhythm.
> The goal is to reach a deeply relaxed state without crossing into full sleep.
